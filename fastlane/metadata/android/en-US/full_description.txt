SecureCall provides end-to-end encrypted one-to-one voice calls through a SecureID. A conventional account is not required for the SecureID calling path.

Features:
* XChaCha20-Poly1305 application-frame encryption
* Per-call X25519 and HKDF-SHA256 key derivation
* Opus audio with Wi-Fi and mobile-data support
* Optional local contacts for displaying names
* Incoming-call notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging
* STEALTH-DELETE emergency local-data wipe
* Public source and current audit status

The signaling service does not store call content. It processes limited routing and delivery metadata as described in the privacy policy.

The Google Play edition does not provide or install a VPN service. It follows Android's active network, including a VPN managed by Android or another trusted app.

The current Android app contains no WalletConnect or in-app IFR-token unlock. Paid controls remain unavailable until their separate product and finance release gates pass.
