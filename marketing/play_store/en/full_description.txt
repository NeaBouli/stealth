SecureCall — Your voice belongs to you.

SecureCall is the first phone app built from the ground up for maximum privacy. Every call is end-to-end encrypted with military-grade cryptography that even intelligence agencies cannot break.

🔒 SECURITY AT THE HIGHEST LEVEL

• End-to-end encryption with XChaCha20-Poly1305 (AEAD)
• Key exchange via X25519 (Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman)
• Double Ratchet protocol for Perfect Forward Secrecy
• Every call uses a unique session key
• Cryptography engine written entirely in Rust — memory-safe, no buffer overflows
• Independently audited

📞 CRYSTAL-CLEAR CALLS

• Opus audio codec for natural voice quality
• Optimized for low latency, even on poor connections
• Works over WiFi and cellular networks

🛡️ ZERO METADATA

• No call logs on our servers
• No contact list uploads
• No tracking, no ads
• Your IP address is masked through GhostNet relays

📖 SOURCE AVAILABLE

The complete source code is available on GitHub. Anyone can verify the security — trust through transparency.

TECHNICAL DETAILS

Encryption: XChaCha20-Poly1305 (AEAD)
Key Exchange: X25519 (ECDH)
Forward Secrecy: Double Ratchet Protocol
Audio: Opus Codec (6-510 kbps)
Transport: WebRTC/QUIC with DTLS
Crypto Engine: Rust (memory-safe)
Network: GhostNet P2P Relay (Premium)
