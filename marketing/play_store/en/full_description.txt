SecureCall - encrypted voice calls for Android

SecureCall provides end-to-end encrypted one-to-one voice calls through a SecureID. A conventional account is not required for the SecureID calling path.

SECURITY DESIGN

* XChaCha20-Poly1305 protects application media frames.
* X25519 and HKDF-SHA256 derive separate key material for each call.
* Per-call key material is discarded when the call ends.
* The cryptographic engine is implemented in Rust.
* Call content is not stored by the signaling service.

SecureCall does not claim to implement a Double Ratchet, authenticated identity-key exchange, post-compromise security, or protection from a malicious signaling service. The source and current audit status are available for independent review.

CALLING

* Opus audio codec
* Wi-Fi and mobile-data support
* Incoming-call notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging
* Optional local contacts for displaying names
* Dark theme and accessibility support

PRIVACY

* No contact-list upload
* No call-content storage on the signaling service
* Routing and delivery metadata is processed as described in the privacy policy.
* The Google Play edition does not provide or install a VPN service. It follows Android's active network, including a VPN managed by Android or another trusted app.

PERMISSIONS

* Microphone: required during voice calls
* Internet: required for signaling and media transport
* Notifications: used for incoming calls
* Contacts: optional, used locally to display names

The current Android app contains no WalletConnect or in-app IFR-token unlock. Any future paid offer is separate from this free Google Play edition and remains subject to the availability shown by the seller.
