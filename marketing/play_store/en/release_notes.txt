This update prepares SecureCall for current Android releases:

* Targets Android API 36
* Uses Google Play Billing Library 8.2.1 while paid controls remain disabled
* Improves edge-to-edge layout handling
* Keeps the Google Play build free of an app-owned VPN service
* Improves call stability and notification handling
* Clarifies the implemented per-call encryption design
