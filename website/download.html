FAQ

Which APK should I download?
Use Google Play for the Free store edition. For a direct purchase, download the APK matching your Free, Pro, or Premium entitlement. Each APK supports the packaged Android CPU variants.
Why does only the direct Premium APK include WireGuard?
The Play edition intentionally excludes an app-owned VPN service to meet Google Play's VpnService distribution requirements for this listing. Paid feature unlocks do not add VPN code. Direct Premium is distributed separately and can offer an optional app-managed WireGuard tunnel after Android consent.
Do I need a Pro/Premium license key?
Pro and Premium are activated through a valid subscription or activation code. IFR holders can verify a browser wallet for the seller-set discount displayed at checkout, not an in-app wallet unlock.
How do I verify the APK signature?
All builds are signed with the SecureCall release keystore. Check the latest GitHub release for SHA256 checksums.
