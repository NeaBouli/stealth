Overview

What is a Custom Call ID?

By default, SecureCall assigns a random alphanumeric ID to each device (e.g., android-bc2b28d9). With a Custom Call ID, you choose your own identity — a short, memorable name that others can dial directly.

  • Your identity: Replace your random ID with something personal (e.g., marco, ghost, viper)
  • Premium only: Custom IDs are exclusively available to Premium users
  • Password protected: Your ID is secured by a password you choose at purchase
  • One device at a time: Your ID is active on exactly one device. Transfer it anytime using your password
  • Lifetime ownership: Once purchased, your ID is yours forever (bound to your password)
Pricing

Allowed characters: Lowercase letters (a–z), digits (0–9), and hyphens (-). Must start and end with a letter or digit.
How It Works

Step-by-Step

  1. Choose your ID — Enter your desired ID in the generator below. The system checks availability and shows the price in real time.
  2. Set a password — Choose a strong password (minimum 8 characters). This password is your only way to transfer or recover the ID.
  3. Pay via Stripe — Complete the one-time payment securely through Stripe (card, Klarna, SEPA).
  4. Scan the QR code — After payment, a QR code appears containing your activation deep link.
  5. Open with SecureCall — Scan the QR code with your device camera or tap it on mobile. SecureCall activates your Custom ID instantly.
Device migration: To move your ID to a new device, enter your ID + password on the new device. The old device is automatically deactivated.

Deep Link Format

Technical Details

After payment, a signed deep link is generated:

securecall://custom-id?id=marco&token=SIGNED_TOKEN

The token is a server-signed JWT that proves the purchase. The SecureCall app validates this token, then activates the Custom ID on your device.

Important Notes

Terms & Conditions

  • Password lost = ID lost. We cannot recover your password or transfer your ID without it. There is no support for forgotten passwords.
  • Refunds follow the checkout terms and applicable consumer law. A lost password or ID cannot be technically recovered.
  • IDs are bound to passwords, not persons. Anyone with the password can transfer the ID to their device.
  • Premium required. Custom IDs only work on devices with an active Premium subscription or lifetime license.
  • Offensive IDs may be removed without refund at our discretion.
  • Privacy by design: Your ID is stored as HMAC-SHA256 hash with a server-side secret. Neither the ID nor your password exist in cleartext on our servers. Full Terms of Service.
