User Manual

Complete guide to using SecureCall.

SecureCall user manual — installation, contacts, calls, settings, security, and troubleshooting · Last updated April 1, 2026

Getting Started

Installation & Setup

Get SecureCall running on your device in under two minutes.

  • Install from Play Store Beta — join the beta program on Google Play, or download the APK directly from GitHub.
  • Complete onboarding — swipe through the 4 onboarding screens to learn the basics.
  • Enter your phone number — when prompted, enter your phone number to create your SecureCall ID.
  • Grant permissions — allow Microphone (required for calls) and Notifications (required for incoming call alerts).
Contacts

Managing Contacts

SecureCall uses its own contact list — separate from your phone contacts — to protect your privacy.

  • Add contact manually — go to the Contacts tab, search by phone number, and add the contact.
  • Add via Invite Link — share your personal invite link: stealthx.tech/invite/YOUR-ID. Recipients tap the link to add you automatically.
  • Add via QR Code — go to Contacts → Invite → Show QR Code. The other person scans it with their SecureCall app.
  • Verify contact — long-press a contact → “Verify Contact”. Verified contacts display a ✓ badge confirming their identity.
  • Block contact — long-press a contact → “Block Contact”. Blocked contacts cannot call you.
  • Delete contact — long-press a contact → “Delete Contact”. This removes the contact from your list permanently.
Making Calls

Voice Calls

All calls are encrypted end-to-end. No one — not even SecureCall — can listen to your conversations.

  • Outgoing call — open the Dialer tab, enter the phone number or select a contact, and tap the Call button.
  • Incoming call — when a call arrives, tap Accept to answer or Decline to reject.
  • During a call — use the on-screen buttons: Mute (toggle microphone), Speaker (toggle loudspeaker), and End Call (hang up).
  • No audio? — check your volume level, disable any active VPN, and try switching from Mobile Data to WiFi (or vice versa).
Settings

App Settings

Configure SecureCall to match your security needs and preferences.

  • Account — view your current tier (Free / Pro / Premium) and upgrade using an activation code.
  • Security — TLS security and device attestation status (read-only).
  • Anti-Recording — screenshot blocking, exclusive microphone access, and recording detection. Protects against screen recording and spy apps.
  • Calls — background service toggle (keeps app alive for incoming calls) and call history on/off.
  • Appearance — switch between Dark, Light, or System theme mode.
  • Network — view active network information and set your preferred network type.
  • VPN — configure WireGuard VPN for encrypted network tunneling (Premium tier only).
  • Diagnostics — enable SecLog diagnostic logging, export logs as CSV, or clear all logs (Pro and Premium tiers).
  • Emergency Delete — tap the Emergency Delete button 5 times rapidly to wipe all SecureCall data from your device instantly.
Security

Security Features

SecureCall is built with a zero-trust security model. Every layer is designed to protect your privacy.

  • End-to-end encryption — all calls are encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305, a military-grade authenticated cipher.
  • X25519 key exchange — new encryption keys are generated for every single call. Previous calls cannot be decrypted even if a key is compromised.
  • Verified badge — confirms the identity of your contact. Verify contacts in person by comparing fingerprints.
  • Emergency Delete — destroys all SecureCall data (keys, contacts, call history, settings) in under one second.
  • Anti-Recording — detects screen recording apps, spy software, and unauthorized microphone access in real time.
Troubleshooting

Common Issues

Quick fixes for the most common problems. For detailed troubleshooting, see the Troubleshooting Guide.

  • No audio during call — raise the media volume on your device, disable any active VPN, and try switching between WiFi and Mobile Data.
  • Call disconnected — check your WiFi or Mobile Data connection. If the connection dropped, tap the Reconnect button to re-establish the call.
  • Contact not found — verify the SecureCall ID format is correct. The contact must have SecureCall installed and registered.
  • Ads during call — upgrade to Pro tier to remove ads completely. On the Free tier, ads are paused automatically during active calls.
