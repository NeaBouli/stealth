IFR Token Unlock

Lock $IFR tokens for lifetime Pro or Premium access · Powered by Inferno

Overview

What is IFR?

$IFR (Inferno) is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum. SecureCall integrates with the IFRLock smart contract to offer a unique unlock mechanism: lock your IFR tokens, and gain lifetime access to Pro or Premium features — as long as your tokens remain locked.

Your tokens stay yours. They are never spent or transferred. You can unlock them at any time, but your SecureCall tier will revert to Free when you do.

  • Token: $IFR (Inferno) — 9 decimals
  • Network: Ethereum Mainnet (Chain ID: 1)
  • Lock Contract: 0x769928aBDfc949D0718d8766a1C2d7dBb63954Eb
  • Learn more: ifrunit.tech
How It Works

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Download SecureCall FREE — Get the app from our website or Google Play. The Free tier includes E2E encrypted calls.
  2. Get IFR tokens — Acquire $IFR on a decentralized exchange or through the Inferno bootstrap.
  3. Lock tokens in IFRLock — Use the Inferno dApp to lock your IFR tokens in the IFRLock smart contract on Ethereum.
  4. Open SecureCall Settings — Navigate to the IFR Token Unlock section.
  5. Enter your wallet address — Paste the Ethereum wallet address that holds the locked IFR tokens.
  6. Tap "Verify Lock" — SecureCall checks the IFRLock contract on Ethereum to confirm your locked balance.
  7. Enjoy Pro or Premium — Features unlock instantly based on your locked amount. The app restarts to apply the new tier.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I unlock my tokens?

Yes, at any time. Your IFR tokens are never spent or transferred — only locked in the smart contract. When you unlock them, your SecureCall tier reverts to Free within 24 hours (the re-verification interval).

What if I add more tokens?

If your locked balance crosses a higher threshold (e.g., from 1,000 to 5,000 IFR), tap "Verify Lock" again in Settings. Your tier will upgrade to Premium immediately.

Is it safe?

SecureCall never has access to your private keys or wallet. Verification is read-only — the server calls a view function on the smart contract (no gas cost, no transaction). Your wallet address is stored locally on your device.

Can I use the same wallet on multiple devices?

Manual wallet entry is limited to one device per wallet address to prevent abuse. WalletConnect support (coming soon) will allow verified multi-device access.

What happens if the Ethereum network is down?

Your current tier is cached locally. If re-verification fails due to network issues, your tier remains active until the next successful check. Only a confirmed "insufficient balance" result reverts your tier.

Manual Wallet Entry

Entering Your Wallet Address

  1. Open SecureCall and go to Settings (gear icon)
  2. Scroll to the IFR Token Unlock section
  3. Tap Wallet Address and paste your Ethereum address (starts with 0x, 42 characters)
  4. Tap OK to save
  5. Tap Verify Lock
  6. Wait for Ethereum verification (5–15 seconds)
  7. If successful, the app shows your locked amount and tier, then restarts
Important: Make sure to enter the correct wallet address. The address must be the one that holds locked IFR in the IFRLock contract, not just IFR in your regular balance.
