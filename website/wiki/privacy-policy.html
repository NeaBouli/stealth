SecureCall privacy policy summary · Last updated October 2026

Canonical Policy

One Canonical Privacy Policy

The canonical and effective SecureCall Privacy Policy is maintained at stealthx.tech/privacy.html (effective February 18, 2026, last updated October 15, 2026, available in seven languages). This wiki page is a summary only; where any wording differs, the canonical page applies.

Summary

Data Handling at a Glance

  • Call content is end-to-end encrypted (XChaCha20-Poly1305 over a WebRTC DataChannel/DTLS transport, per-call X25519 keys) and is never stored on servers.
  • Contact lists stay on your device. Optional SecureCall-user discovery sends SHA-256 hashes of normalized phone numbers, and calling by phone number sends that number once to the server for lookup.
  • Free tier shows ads (Google AdMob) and collects anonymous crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, on by default, opt-out in Settings). Pro/Premium show no ads; the Crashlytics SDK is present in these builds but crash collection is disabled at runtime.
  • All tiers use Firebase Cloud Messaging for push and STUN/TURN for connectivity; IP addresses are processed transiently during signaling and not persistently logged.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; details are in the canonical policy.
GDPR rights. Access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, and complaint rights are described on the canonical page. Privacy inquiries are answered within 30 days.
